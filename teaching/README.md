# A proposed teaching sequence

Use the [chapter index](../chapters/README.md) in order. Begin with a spoken units check, derive the two profit lines, then interrupt the worked example with a separate financing equation so learners do not mingle dates or balances. In the next meeting ask two groups to evaluate the rival plans using mean substitution or full scenarios, then have them explain why the recommendations differ. End with the [worksheet](../practice/planning-worksheet.md), the individual transfer and the [scoring criteria](../practice/answer-key.md).

The [proposed curriculum Blueprint](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/ai-plus-one-paper/blob/main/book-queue/editorial-blueprints/19-mba-math-ai-one.md) sets the five-topic scope. This sequence is a draft suggestion, not an adopted syllabus or a graded LMS import. The optional AI activity is feedback on a prior attempt; use only invented data. Adopt the course's own disclosure and assessment rules before teaching it.
