## Suggested scoring

Give 0 for missing or materially wrong, 1 for partially supported, and 2 for correct and reproducible on each dimension: unit consistency; break-even and caps; scenario weighting and comparison; financing assumptions; a feasible decision and check; evidence of revision with assistance disclosed; and the independent transfer. Credit a different memo if the declared decision rule and constraints support it.
