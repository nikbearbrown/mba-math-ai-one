# MBA Math (AI Plus One)

## About the book

A repair service has two ways to staff next month. The cheaper plan meets its minimum profit target, but the other plan earns more on average. This compact **rough draft** teaches MBA entrants how to turn rates, costs and probabilities into a decision they can explain and check. It assumes basic arithmetic and familiarity with simple tables. It needs no calculus or paid tool and has not had an expert final edit.

## Start reading

Start with [Give every rate a denominator](chapters/01-give-every-rate-a-denominator.md) and follow the [complete chapter index](chapters/README.md). The Markdown here is the reading edition. No formatted export or separate web edition is available.

## Chapter guide

**Part I: Build the quantities**

1. [Give every rate a denominator](chapters/01-give-every-rate-a-denominator.md). Translate daily rates to monthly requests and show what a denominator measures.
2. [Find the break-even booking](chapters/02-find-the-break-even-booking.md). Build revenue and cost lines and check the first feasible whole booking count.
3. [Keep the time units](chapters/03-keep-the-time-units.md). Contrast simple and compound balances without mixing financing and operations.

**Part II: Choose under uncertainty**

4. [Weight the outcomes, not the inputs](chapters/04-weight-outcomes-not-inputs.md). See how applying a capacity limit changes the expectation.
5. [Screen plans before ranking them](chapters/05-screen-plans-before-ranking.md). Select a feasible plan and test what would change the decision.

## How to teach or study it

Work the units, then the formulas, then the choice. Predict a result on paper before trying the [case checks](examples/README.md). The [proposed teaching sequence](teaching/README.md) links the Blueprint and explains how to assess the [one-month plan](practice/planning-worksheet.md). Use the [answer key](practice/answer-key.md) after the first attempt and keep the new transfer problem unaided.
